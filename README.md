### Kyle Johnson

AI, data science, and software in Richmond, Virginia.

Marine Corps sergeant, then computer science and statistics at Columbia, then six years as a data scientist at Meta. In 2024 I founded [Monument Labs](https://monumentlabs.io), a small studio that keeps a few AI products running. Today I work on AI strategy and architecture inside a large enterprise, and I build small tools in the evenings.

**Things here worth a look**

- [sculptor](https://github.com/GKjohns/sculptor): an AI carves a block of virtual marble while you watch. The model plans each pass, code turns it into chisel strikes on a 5.6-million-voxel block, and there is no undo.
- [project-gpe](https://github.com/GKjohns/project-gpe): Proofmap, a reading site for the 373 result families in OpenAI's machine-written math papers. A digested proof becomes a map of named steps in plain English, each linked back to the typeset paper. [Live site](https://digest-dun.vercel.app).
- [holdem-ai](https://github.com/GKjohns/holdem-ai): seven AI models play No-Limit Hold'em on identical cards, scored with confidence intervals. [Write-up and live table](https://www.kylejohnson.ai/blog/ai-poker-night).
- [voice-world](https://github.com/GKjohns/voice-world) and [instant-interiors](https://github.com/GKjohns/instant-interiors): 3D spaces in the browser that you change by talking. [onair](https://github.com/GKjohns/onair) is the recorder I film the demos with: camera and browser tab composited into a vertical reel, then captions and music.
- [gokyo-poster](https://github.com/GKjohns/gokyo-poster): the 40 Kodokan judo throws, each paired with an everyday scene that explains its physics. Live at [gentleway.ink](https://www.gentleway.ink).
- [2048-ai](https://github.com/GKjohns/2048-ai): nine models, same seeded games, compared on score, speed, and cost.
- [photo-curator](https://github.com/GKjohns/photo-curator): a Claude Code skill that culls a camera card and edits the keepers.
- [scikit-learn #9701](https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn/pull/9701): taught `check_estimator` to test estimators that take precomputed kernels. Shipped in 0.20.
